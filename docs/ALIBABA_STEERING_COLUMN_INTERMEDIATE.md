# Alibaba steering column / intermediate-shaft sourcing screen

Research current as of **2026-09-01**.

This document closes a remaining Phase-5 steering hardware gap around the existing VolksMule rule:

> **The steering path remains mechanically continuous. EPS assists it; the computer does not own it.**

The remaining hardware question is the mechanical path between the steering wheel and rack:

- collapsible upper column;
- tilt/adjustment mechanism if used;
- bearings/support housing;
- intermediate shaft;
- U-joints/couplers;
- firewall/seal interface;
- splines/clamps;
- crash-collapse behavior.

---

## 1. Central result

Treat the steering path as two sourcing layers:

### Safety-critical upper column

Use either:

1. a proven mass-market **collapsible donor steering-column assembly**, or
2. an automotive steering-column manufacturer with documented collapse/crash behavior.

### Geometry/service intermediate shaft

Use a conventional telescoping/intermediate shaft with ordinary U-joints and clamp/spline interfaces, ideally from a common existing application or a supplier capable of producing a shaft to a controlled drawing.

> **Do not buy a universal racing column and assume “collapsible” means vehicle-crash validated.**

Alibaba repeatedly surfaces Yuhuan King Machinery / Kingshaft for:

### Prototype rule

Prefer a common existing shaft family if its:

- spline interfaces;
- collapsed/extended length;
- U-joint operating angles;
- telescoping travel;
- clamp geometry;
- corrosion protection;

fit the Mule.

A custom-length shaft is acceptable if it retains common U-joints/splines.

## 4. Crash-collapse boundary

The steering column is part of the occupant-crash problem.

The system must be evaluated with:

- steering wheel / airbag;
- driver H-point;
- column angle;
- instrument panel;
- firewall/toe-board intrusion;
- pedal motion;
- seat/belt/restraint geometry;
- column support bracket breakaway/collapse behavior.

Therefore:

> **The upper column cannot be frozen independently from the restraint and safety-cell package.**

A catalog claim of “collapsible” is not enough.

Need exact evidence for the selected upper column:

- collapse stroke;
- collapse force curve;
- breakaway bracket behavior;
- tilt/telescope lock strength;
- torsional strength;
- bending stiffness;
- bearing retention;
- spline/clamp retention;
- applicable crash validation / OEM application history.

---

## 5. Mechanical serviceability

Preferred column/shaft design should permit:

- steering wheel removal with ordinary tools;
- column removal without removing the windshield;
- separate intermediate-shaft replacement;
- visible pinch-bolt/clamp retention;
- replaceable firewall boot/seal;
- no software pairing required to restore the mechanical steering path;
- separate replacement of EPS electronics/motor where architecture allows.

If a steering-angle sensor is integrated, its calibration must remain locally accessible.

---

## 6. Geometry rules

Do not freeze a column or shaft until Revision-A places:

- steering-wheel center;
- driver H-point;
- rack input centerline;
- firewall pass-through;
- front crash structure;
- EPS motor/gearbox envelope;
- pedal box.

Then check:

- U-joint angles through adjustment range;
- shaft plunge/telescope reserve;
- no binding through body tolerance/crash deformation;
- no straight intrusion spear aimed at the driver;
- service-removal path;
- heat/water/corrosion exposure.

## 7. BUY / ADAPT / DESIGN verdict

| Part | Verdict |
|---|---|

## 8. Current shortlist

### Upper column

1. **Common donor collapsible column** from a locally supported compact SUV/crossover application — first Prototype-1 path.
2. **Nanjing Chili** — strongest current Chinese road-intent custom/production column lead.
3. **Hubei Hengjiu** — broader steering-system supplier worth retaining as an alternate supplier family.

### Intermediate shaft

1. common local/OE application shaft if geometry works;
2. Yuhuan King/Kingshaft-class replacement/custom shaft;
3. SAIC Maxus / Toyota / Ford-style Alibaba low-MOQ shaft assemblies for packaging/service studies where interfaces fit.

---

## 9. Purchase gate

Do not buy the final upper column until:

- driver package is placed;
- steering-wheel/restraint family is understood;
- firewall/rack axes are modeled;
- collapse stroke/force evidence is available.

Low-cost intermediate shafts may later be useful for bench/package experiments, but no shaft becomes the final interface merely because its length is close.

---

## 10. Current conclusion

The remaining mechanical steering path is fully sourceable without inventing a column.

The correct architecture is:

> **proven collapsible automotive upper column → ordinary serviceable intermediate shaft/U-joints → mechanical rack → EPS assist layered onto that path.**

Alibaba is particularly useful for the intermediate-shaft/service layer. The upper crash-collapse column stays in the donor/Tier-1-quality layer.
