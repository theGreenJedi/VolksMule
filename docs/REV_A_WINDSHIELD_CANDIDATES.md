# Revision-A windshield candidates

Research date: 2026-08-31

This document narrows the first real windshield envelopes for **VolksMule Prototype 1**.

It exists because the windshield strongly constrains:

- A-pillar position;
- cowl height;
- roof leading edge;
- driver eye point and natural visibility;
- wiper sweep and linkage geometry;
- defrost duct geometry;
- dashboard depth;
- future forward-AEB camera location;
- body opening fabrication and replacement strategy.

The project rule is:

> **Use a common compliant windshield if the body can reasonably adapt to it. Custom glass is the fallback, not the starting point.**

The candidate dimensions below are useful **packaging/bounding dimensions**, not CAD surfaces. Final body tooling requires actual glass or supplier geometry, including curvature, edge shape, frit, molding, urethane bond path, local thickness and allowable installation tolerances.

---

# Selection criteria

A good VolksMule windshield should score well on all of these:

1. **North-American replacement depth** — the owner should not be stranded because a niche windshield vanished.
2. **Simple variant structure** — avoid a body aperture that only works with one ADAS/heated/rain-sensor SKU.
3. **Useful vertical view** — VolksMule values natural visibility.
4. **Moderate width** — enough glass for sight lines without forcing excessive body width.
5. **Conventional bonded installation** — ordinary urethane/molding/service procedures are preferred.
6. **No required embedded electronics** — sensors may mount behind the glass later; they should not own the glass architecture.
7. **Wiper-friendly geometry** — useful swept area with ordinary arms/linkage.
8. **Body fit** — must coexist with roof crash structure, A-pillars, dashboard, defrost, restraints and the desired compact vehicle envelope.

# Candidate 1 — Honda CR-V 2002–2006 family

## NAGS family

- **FW02294** / tint variants
- approximately **1539 × 817 mm** from a current XYG replacement listing
- roughly **60.6 × 32.2 in** from that measured replacement listing
- historical NAGS catalog transcriptions commonly place this family at approximately **60 × 34 in** overall bounding size depending on catalog convention/variant

## Why it is strong

This is currently the **leading Honda candidate**.

The 2002–2004/06 CR-V windshield family has several virtues:

- compact-SUV proportions close to the intended Mule scale;
- no integrated heating or required sensor hardware in the basic FW02294 family;
- broad glass area without becoming a panoramic styling part;
- contemporary replacement sources still identify the NAGS family;
- the surrounding vehicle architecture was designed for upright utility visibility rather than a low sports-car cowl.

A current 2026 technical replacement reference identifies FW02294 and states that the basic glass has **no integrated sensors or heating elements**.

## Current source evidence

- https://www.go-parts.com/garage/windshield-glass-honda-cr-v-2002-2004
- https://provetro.com.ua/lobovoe-steklo-honda-cr-v-2002-2006-2-xyg
- https://www.genioshop.com.br/parabrisa-honda-crv-2002-a-2006-veltrio

## Cautions

- Japan/UK production differences affected moldings and related installation parts on the original CR-V.
- Published dimensions are bounding/retail dimensions, not the full curved installation surface.
- Need actual sample or CAD/profile tracing before the body opening is frozen.

## Verdict

**PRIMARY REV-A STUDY CANDIDATE.**

# Candidate 2 — Nissan Xterra / Frontier / Pathfinder family

## NAGS family

- **FW02510**
- later related variant **FW03866**
- NAGS catalog data: approximately **33.1 × 60.2 in**

## Multi-model fit advantage

Current replacement listings identify FW02510 across multiple high-volume utility vehicles, including:

- Nissan Xterra;
- Nissan Frontier;
- Nissan Pathfinder;
- some related Navara/Suzuki Equator applications depending on market.

That interchange breadth is strategically valuable. A windshield shared across multiple trucks/SUVs has a better chance of remaining available for decades than a one-model specialty part.

## Current 2026 supply evidence

A current Fuyao listing identifies **FW02510GBN** for 2005–2015 Xterra/Frontier/Pathfinder and shows it in stock. A separate 2026 owner report documents a Vitro FW02510 replacement installed in July 2026, demonstrating active contemporary supply from more than one glass manufacturer.

Sources:

- https://montiglass.com/fw02510gbn
- https://roxfinder.com/blog/xterra-glass-visor-height
- https://www.ebay.com/itm/117083985105

## Why it may beat the CR-V glass

- excellent multi-model interchange;
- straightforward truck/SUV geometry;
- no architecture requirement for embedded ADAS electronics;
- currently supplied by major glass manufacturers such as Fuyao and Vitro;
- dimensions are close to the CR-V candidate, making both easy to compare in one packaging model.

## Cautions

- shade-band/visor treatment varies by manufacturer and replacement revision;
- exact frit/molding/bond geometry needs inspection;
- still need a physical sample or accurate edge/curvature data before body freeze.

## Verdict

**PRIMARY REV-A STUDY CANDIDATE — likely strongest long-term supply argument.**

# Candidate 3 — Honda CR-V 1997–2001

## NAGS

- **FW02023**
- historical NAGS size: approximately **32.5 × 60.5 in**

## Why it remains attractive

This is philosophically very close to VolksMule:

- simple first-generation compact-SUV glass;
- large useful sight line;
- no ADAS/sensor dependency;
- conventional bonded windshield;
- replacement stock still exists in 2026.

Current replacement source:

- https://executivemobileglassshop.com/product/front-windshield-glass-honda-cr-v-1997-2001-4dr-utility-replacement/

Historical NAGS reference:

- https://www.scribd.com/document/668415912/Catalogo-Nags-1990-1999-Stob

## Concern

Long-term supply depth is likely not as strong as the later Nissan multi-model family or second-generation CR-V family. It remains an excellent geometry/control candidate but should not win solely because the first-gen CR-V is the project reference vehicle.

## Verdict

**SECONDARY / CONTROL CANDIDATE.**

# Candidate 4 — Honda Element 2003–2011

## NAGS

- **FW02380**
- approximately **28.6 × 59.7 in** in NAGS data
- current XYG listing gives **1522 × 725 mm**, which agrees closely

Sources:

- https://executivemobileglassshop.com/product/front-windshield-honda-element-4dr-utility-replacement-and-installation/
- https://tov54.ru/catalog/after_crush/stekla/lobovie/xyg_fw02380lfw_x.html

## Strengths

- very simple utility-vehicle glass;
- broad width;
- still actively available;
- straightforward vehicle architecture.

## Weakness

The shorter vertical dimension works against VolksMule's visibility goal. It may force a higher cowl or a roof/cabin relationship we do not want.

## Verdict

**KEEP AS PACKAGING COMPARISON; NOT CURRENT LEADER.**

# Candidate 5 — Honda CR-V 2012–2016 basic family

## NAGS

- base family **FW03516**
- later generation branches include mirror, rain-sensor, heated-wiper and LDWS variants

Current replacement supply exists in 2026.

Sources:

- https://executivemobileglassshop.com/product/front-windshield-glass-honda-cr-v-2012-2016-4dr-utility-replacement/
- https://windshieldreplacementdb.blogspot.com/2020/02/2012-to-2016-honda-cr-v-auto-glass-parts.html

## Strength

Large modern visibility envelope and excellent replacement ecosystem.

## Weakness

The family already demonstrates the variant explosion VolksMule wants to avoid: sensor brackets, LDWS options, heated park zones and mirror variants. The base FW03516 is simple enough for a geometry study, but this generation is less attractive as the long-term architecture anchor.

## Verdict

**BENCHMARK ONLY unless its geometry proves dramatically superior.**

# Revision-A ranking

Carry these three first:

| Rank | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1A** | **Honda CR-V FW02294** | excellent compact-SUV geometry, simple basic glass, strong current replacement path |
| **1B** | **Nissan FW02510** | very strong multi-model replacement ecosystem and simple truck/SUV architecture |
| **3** | **Honda CR-V FW02023** | simple and philosophically ideal, but older supply ecosystem |

Use Element FW02380 and later CR-V FW03516 as comparison envelopes rather than leading candidates.

No winner is frozen yet.

# What Revision-A CAD should do

Model the first three candidate envelopes against the same driver/roof/cowl package.

For each candidate, record:

- glass bounding width/height;
- provisional glass rake;
- cowl location;
- roof-header location;
- A-pillar base/top positions;
- driver eye-to-glass sight lines;
- near-field road visibility;
- dashboard depth;
- defrost outlet path;
- wiper motor/linkage zone;
- approximate swept area;
- rear-view mirror mount zone;
- future AEB camera clear optical zone;
- roof/side crash-structure conflict;
- weather/molding/service access;
- replacement/install clearance.

## Important

Do **not** make an AEB-camera bracket part of the windshield selection yet. The windshield should first win as compliant, serviceable glass. The dedicated forward-safety module can be mounted behind an appropriate clear zone later if optical requirements allow.

# Physical validation plan

Before freezing the body aperture:

1. Obtain or borrow one physical windshield from the leading family.
2. Trace the perimeter onto a large fixture/template.
3. Measure curvature/crown at defined stations.
4. Document glass thickness and edge treatment.
5. Record frit/bond path.
6. Record molding/clip requirements.
7. Sweep candidate wiper arms physically or in CAD.
8. Confirm available replacement manufacturers and NAGS cross-reference.
9. Repeat with the second-place glass if the packaging decision is close.

A damaged but dimensionally intact take-out windshield is acceptable for this packaging exercise and may be nearly free from a local auto-glass shop.

---

# Architecture rule created by this study

> **The windshield is a service part, not styling sculpture.**

VolksMule should shape its cowl, roof and pillars around a durable replacement ecosystem when the resulting visibility and structure are good. A custom windshield must demonstrate a meaningful whole-vehicle benefit before it is allowed to create a permanent bespoke supply dependency.

---

# Current decision

**Do not order custom glass.**

Move FW02294, FW02510 and FW02023 into Revision-A packaging simultaneously. Select the winner only after sight-line, structure, wiper, AEB optical-zone and service studies are compared.
