# Alibaba body-service hardware — wipers, manual windows, lamps, mirrors and door mechanisms

Research current as of **2026-09-01**.

This document continues the VolksMule roadmap sourcing mission into the ordinary body-service hardware that should remain cheap, tactile, replaceable and understandable.

The project rule is:

> **Mechanical/simple hardware wins unless electrification or integration clearly earns its keep.**

The computer does not own access, visibility or weather protection.

---

## 1. Wiper motor and linkage

### Preferred sourcing mode: BUY / ADAPT

Alibaba currently exposes strong 12-V automotive wiper-motor and linkage manufacturing depth.

### Current lead — Zhejiang Jixiang Automobile Parts

Current Alibaba supplier material shows:

- 12-V automotive wiper motors;
- window motors/regulators;
- blower motors and radiator fans;
- long marketplace history;
- IATF 16949 shown on current supplier profile;
- North-American export activity;
- customization/ODM capability.

This is a plausible supplier family for a conventional two-speed/self-park wiper motor once the windshield and linkage geometry are fixed.

### Linkage alternate — Xiamen Kction Auto Parts

Current Alibaba material shows complete wiper motors/linkages and lists IATF 16949 on the supplier profile.

### VolksMule requirement

The final system should use:

- ordinary 12-V motor;
- physical stalk/switch command;
- low/high/intermittent control that does not depend on infotainment;
- self-park function;
- replaceable linkage pivots/bushings;
- accessible motor and transmission mechanism;
- conventional blade/arm attachment;
- no proprietary windshield-specific smart-wiper ECU unless it earns its keep.

### Selection blockers

Exact motor/linkage waits for:

- final windshield family/curvature;
- cowl depth;
- pivot spacing;
- required wiped area;
- blade length;
- parking position;
- linkage load/torque.

### Verdict

> **GREEN commodity supplier category. Zhejiang Jixiang-class 12-V motor plus conventional serviceable linkage is the preferred architecture.**

## 2. Washer system

### Preferred sourcing mode: BUY commodity

Alibaba exposes ordinary:

- 12-V washer pumps;
- reservoirs;
- level sensors;
- hose;
- check valves;
- spray nozzles.

No dedicated exotic supplier relationship is required.

### VolksMule rule

Use:

- separate removable reservoir;
- common 12-V pump;
- visible/accessible hose routing;
- replaceable nozzles;
- physical stalk command;
- local low-fluid indication if desired.

Reservoir geometry can be custom/local because it is low-risk packaging plastic; the pump/nozzle ecosystem should remain commodity.

### Verdict

> **LOCAL-FIRST / ALIBABA-OPTIONAL commodity hardware.**

## 3. Manual window regulators

### Preferred sourcing mode: BUY / ADAPT mechanical

Alibaba has substantial existing manual-regulator inventory for older passenger vehicles, trucks and utility vehicles, including current listings for Defender-, Isuzu-, GM- and commercial-vehicle applications.

Typical current listing prices are in the single-digit to tens-of-dollars range, confirming that a mechanical crank mechanism is not an expensive special request.

### Why this matters

This validates the Prototype-1 baseline:

> **Manual windows are normal, cheap, and fully sourceable.**

They avoid:

- window motors;
- high-current door wiring;
- anti-pinch electronics;
- door control modules;
- powered-window regulatory complexity;
- one more failure that traps the glass in a bad position.

### Current supplier paths

Alibaba currently exposes:

- Ruian Eachin Automobile Parts manual regulators;
- Ningbo Tower Machinery custom/manual winder mechanisms;
- multiple existing utility/truck manual regulator families;
- specialist window-regulator suppliers including Jiangxi Dellsun and Qeepei for mixed manual/power catalogs.

### Selection strategy

Prefer an **existing common mechanical regulator geometry** if door/glass architecture can accept it.

If final glass travel requires custom geometry, design the door around:

- standard crank spindle/handle interface;
- common cable/scissor mechanism concepts;
- replaceable sliders/rollers;
- mechanical upper/lower stops;
- accessible fasteners.

### Verdict

> **GREEN mechanical category / manual remains Prototype-1 baseline.**

Power windows remain optional convenience equipment, not required architecture.

## 4. Door latches and mechanical locks

### Preferred sourcing mode: proven application family / system-level validation

Alibaba has thousands of door-latch and actuator listings, but road-intent door retention is not a place to choose a random low-cost mechanism.

Prototype 1 requires:

- mechanical exterior handle path;
- mechanical interior release;
- keyed/manual lock path;
- door must remain openable without 12-V power;
- latch/striker/hinge system must satisfy the completed vehicle's FMVSS 206 requirements.

### Sourcing strategy

First preference:

1. common high-volume donor latch/striker family with known replacement depth;
2. adapt door structure to that proven mechanism if geometry remains sensible;
3. Alibaba may supply replacement equivalents or later production alternates only when exact strength/durability/traceability evidence exists.

Generic powered latch/actuator assemblies are not an improvement merely because they add electronics.

### Verdict

> **DONOR / ADAPT first. Alibaba is supplier intelligence and alternate manufacturing, not permission to invent an unvalidated latch system.**

## 5. Hinges

### Preferred sourcing mode: proven automotive hinge or drawing-based fabrication

The hinge is mechanically simple but structurally meaningful.

Exact selection depends on:

- door mass;
- hinge spacing;
- opening angle;
- sag requirement;
- body/door reinforcement;
- corrosion protection;
- crash/retention requirements.

Prefer existing automotive hinge families with replaceable pins/bushings where possible.

If body geometry requires custom hinge leaves, VolksMule owns the geometry and suppliers fabricate to drawing.

### Verdict

> **ADAPT / DESIGN + BUY fabrication. No exotic powered hinge architecture.**

## 6. Exterior mirrors

### Preferred sourcing mode: common manually adjustable/simple donor family

Alibaba has broad mirror supply, including full side-mirror assemblies from high-volume vehicle applications.

Prototype 1 does not require:

- power folding;
- camera mirrors;
- memory;
- puddle lights;
- blind-spot LEDs;
- app configuration.

A simple manually adjusted mirror is acceptable if it meets visibility requirements and remains stable at speed.

### Sourcing strategy

Prefer:

- large conventional glass area;
- common replaceable glass;
- simple foldaway breakaway joint if practical;
- mechanical/manual adjustment or simple fixed mount;
- easy replacement after trail/parking damage.

The final mirror geometry must be checked against FMVSS 111/rear-visibility requirements and body sight lines.

### Verdict

> **LOCAL/DONOR-FIRST; Alibaba useful for replacement-equivalent manufacturing and simple mirror families after geometry is known.**

## 7. Headlamps / front lighting

### Preferred sourcing mode: complete validated optical assembly

Alibaba has many lamp suppliers, including current IATF-claimed manufacturers such as Foshan Tuff Plus Auto Lighting and large complete-headlamp catalogs.

This proves manufacturing depth.

It does **not** make a listing's `DOT` icon sufficient compliance evidence.

### Hard VolksMule rule

> **The compliant object is the complete lamp/optical assembly, not a bulb claim.**

Do not:

- install generic LED retrofit bulbs into reflectors not designed for them;
- rely on marketplace `DOT` text alone;
- create a bespoke lighting signature that makes replacement impossible.

Prefer:

1. an existing high-volume complete headlamp module whose compliance/photometry is known;
2. a standardized round/rectangular compliant module if one packages better;
3. Alibaba manufacturing only when the exact optical assembly has documented test/marking evidence.

### Current supplier intelligence

Tuff Plus is worth retaining as a manufacturing lead because current Alibaba material shows:

- large dedicated lighting factory footprint;
- IATF 16949 claim;
- complete LED headlamp manufacturing;
- DOT/ECE claims;
- OEM/ODM capability.

Road-intent verdict remains documentation-dependent.

### Verdict

> **COMPLETE OPTICAL MODULE ONLY. Local/common compliant assembly first; Alibaba alternate after exact evidence.**

## 8. Tail lamps / marker / turn lamps

Same principle as front lighting.

Prefer complete replaceable modules with:

- documented photometry/markings;
- separate serviceable harness connector;
- ordinary body fastening;
- no body-controller dependency for basic operation.

A conventional 12-V relay/driver output is sufficient where current permits.

### Verdict

> **BUY complete compliant modules; avoid styling-specific one-piece body lamps where possible.**

## 10. Purchase philosophy

These parts should be **easy to replace and boring to diagnose**.

The intended service experience is:

- wiper failed → replace motor/linkage with hand tools;
- window jammed → remove trim and repair a mechanical regulator;
- door will not latch → inspect mechanical linkage/latch/striker;
- mirror broken → unbolt and replace mirror;
- lamp damaged → replace complete module;
- none of those repairs require coding, cloud access, dealer pairing or infotainment boot.
